# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Technical and intellectually curious readers — mostly developers, designers, and self-directed learners. They read on laptops during focused sessions and on phones during commutes. They value depth, clarity, and craft. Many read in both Chinese and English contexts.

## Product Purpose

A personal blog for publishing long-form technical writing, reflections, and life notes. Success means readers finish articles, trust the author's voice, and return for new posts. The design itself is part of the product — it signals care and establishes credibility before a word is read.

## Brand Personality

Scholarly, restrained, deliberate. Quiet confidence over self-promotion. The only fixed ornament is the palm leaf shadow in the upper right (BambooShadow) — living, wind-driven foliage cast on the page. Full visual system is being redesigned from zero (see `docs/design-proposals/`); until a proposal lands, treat the site as transitional — not a magazine, not a startup landing page, not a generic Medium blog.

## Design Language (in redesign)

- **Palm shadow is sacred** — BambooShadow is the only site decoration; no moonlight wash, no yellow light blobs, no second foliage layer.
- **Everything else is open** — color, type, borders, interaction physics: pick via design proposals, then pin here.
- **Anti-slop** — no classical pastiche (seals, vertical scroll kitsch), no Medium cards, no neon crypto glow.

## Anti-references

- Generic Medium-style blogs with giant hero images and thin content
- SaaS landing pages with feature grids, social proof carousels, and aggressive CTAs
- Overly decorative "creative portfolio" sites with heavy scroll animations
- Dark-glow crypto / developer aesthetics (neon accents on pure black)
- Identical card grids with icon + heading + text patterns

## Design Principles

1. **Content first** — typography and spacing serve the text, never compete with it
2. **Restraint over novelty** — avoid trends that will date quickly; favor quiet craftsmanship
3. **Respect the reader's attention** — minimize distractions, maximize readability and focus
4. **Quality should be felt, not announced** — no badges, no trust signals, no "look how designed this is"
5. **Dark mode as a courtesy, not a feature** — both themes should feel native and unobtrusive

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA target
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` throughout
- Support browser/user font-size preferences (no fixed px root)
- Sufficient contrast in both light and dark modes
